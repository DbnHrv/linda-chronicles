Welcome to Group 2 Pharmacy!
Your personalized orientation guide for the Pharmacy Internship Programme
Orientation
Orientation is held on your first day at 8:00 AM in the Main Conference Room (Ground Floor). Please bring two valid IDs and your signed MOA. Dress code is business casual with closed-toe shoes.
Parking & Access
Intern parking is available at Lot B (East Wing). Display your intern parking pass on your dashboard. The main entrance badge system is programmed with your ID within 24 hours of signing your onboarding documents.
ID & Uniform
Your intern ID will be issued on Day 1. Wear your white pharmacy coat at all times in clinical areas. Your full name and "INTERN" label must be visible on your name tag.
